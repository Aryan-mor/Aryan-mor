## Hi, I'm Aryan 👋

I've worked with React and TypeScript for 9 years, from 30+ client websites to the design system and dashboards of an HR platform with 400+ customers.

Most of my work is frontend architecture: design systems, data-heavy dashboards, caching, and SSO with role-based access. I also write the Node.js endpoints behind my features. I like finding what users struggle with, proposing the fix, and shipping it.

I live in Berlin on an EU Blue Card and I'm **open to senior frontend and product engineer roles**, in Berlin or remote in Germany. No sponsorship needed, available at short notice.

**On the side: [TaleSelf](https://taleself.app)**

A language-learning story game where you are the main character. You make choices, and an LLM writes the next story branch at your level, from A1 to C2. 22 languages to learn, 9 app languages including RTL. Generation takes a few seconds, so instead of a spinner the app fills the wait with quick flashcard drills.

Solo project, idea to app, currently in Android closed testing.
Stack: Flutter, TypeScript, Fastify, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Gemini on Vertex AI.
